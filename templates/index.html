Check that a face is real before you trust who it is.

TRUE FACE AI runs a photo through face detection, a liveness check and a deepfake screen, then matches it against the people you have registered.

Verify a face Register a person

Works with your webcam or an uploaded photo. Everything runs on this server; photos are not stored after a check.

What happens in one check

Each photo goes through the same five steps in order. The first step that fails ends the check and tells you why.

  1. Find the face

    A cascade detector locates the largest face in the photo and crops it.

  2. Confirm it is a face

    A second classifier rejects crops that are not faces, such as a poster or a wall.

  3. Test for liveness

    Texture, contrast and glare cues separate a live person from a printed photo or a screen.

  4. Screen for manipulation

    A deepfake score flags swapped, smoothed or warped faces. It is advisory until it is trained on better data.

  5. Match the identity

    The face is compared with the people you registered and a distance score decides the match.

What you can do here

Verify

Use the camera with an oval guide to line up your face, review the captured photo, then run the check.

Register

Take three to eight photos of a person. Each one is checked before it is accepted.

Manage people

Search the registered users and remove anyone, including their stored face samples.

Review activity

The dashboard shows outcomes, daily volume, model accuracy and a log of recent checks.

Built to learn from, not to guard a door

The training data is a small real face set (100 faces from the LFW collection, 25 by 25 pixels). The spoof and deepfake samples are simulated from those faces.

  • The dashboard shows the measured accuracy of each model, with the simulated tests labelled.
  • Liveness is enforced. The deepfake check only advises, because it is weak at this resolution.
  • Replace the files in data/ with real attack footage and retrain before relying on any result.
